Five fixes, in order

Each fix comes with the evidence behind it. The first is open.

1Answer "where can I buy this in Finland" on an ABB page

Write the answer in plain text on the eFinder country pages: which approved distributors sell online, what to expect on stock and lead time, and how to order by part number.

Evidence: distributors are named first in 12 of 22 buying answers. Onninen's Finnish shop is cited in 4 answers and Farnell Finland in 4. ChatGPT names no ABB product at all for the Nordic distributor question. Claude lists ABB 11th of 11 there, and cites the eFinder page.

2Publish a part-number and replacement lookup

One page per breaker family that takes a type designation and returns the replacement, with the data a buyer needs to confirm the match: rated current, poles, breaking capacity, trip unit.

Evidence: for "How do I find the right replacement circuit breaker by part number?", Claude and AI Overviews do not name ABB. The pages Claude cites are Schneider Electric, Eaton and Siemens cross-reference material.

3Give each UPS family a page that states the case

MegaFlex, HiPerGuard and DPA 500: capacity range, efficiency, behaviour under AI load swings, and named Nordic references. The Telia Helsinki project is the one Claude already cites.

Evidence: on "best UPS systems for a large data center", ABB is 4th in ChatGPT and absent from AI Mode, AI Overviews and Claude. On the AI-rack question ChatGPT leaves ABB out and AI Mode ranks it 4th. Vertiv leads that question on all four surfaces.

4Publish ABB's own comparison and selection content

Molded case vs air breaker, soft starter vs drive, ABB vs Schneider Electric vs Siemens. Put the comparison on abb.com so ABB's pages, not a reseller's, are the cited source.

Evidence: ABB leads selection answers, yet a trader's blog (stoklink.com) is cited in 6 answers, and its "Emax 2 vs Masterpact MTZ vs SENTRON" comparison is a source in Claude's breaker answers.

5Re-run this set every month and give each team its slice

Repeat the 20 questions on the four surfaces, add Finnish and Swedish versions, and track three numbers: share of answers naming ABB, position, and answers citing ABB pages. Use a signed-in ChatGPT or the API to capture its sources.

Evidence: one run per question gives a snapshot. A monthly series separates a real change from the normal variation in AI answers.

How this was measured

Questions
20 buyer questions: where to buy (6), product selection (6), data centers (5) and three that name ABB (13, 19, 20). Each was asked once on 19 November 2026.
Surfaces
ChatGPT as a guest with web search. Google AI Mode and AI Overviews from a signed-in Chrome profile in Helsinki, so results lean toward Finland. Claude in three separate sessions with web search and no context about ABB or the asker; each session listed the brands in its own answer.
Position
The order of first mention in the answer text, matched against a list of 47 brands and distributors. It is not always the model's own ranking.
Limits
AI Overviews appeared for 13 of the 17 unbranded questions, so its figures use 13 answers. Answers vary from run to run, so treat single cells as indicative.
Product names
Names that appear in answers, such as Emax 2, MegaFlex and HiPerGuard, count as ABB mentions.
